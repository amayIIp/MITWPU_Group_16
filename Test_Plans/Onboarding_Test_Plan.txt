Test Plan: Onboarding Flow

1. Application Launch
   - Open the app.
   - Verify that the first page animation/splash screen appears.
   - Verify transition to the Login/Sign Up selection screen.

2. Sign Up Flow
   - Tap "Sign Up".
   - Enter a valid name, email, and password.
   - Verify password requirements are checked (if any).
   - Tap submit/create account.
   - Verify successful account creation and transition to Onboarding Instructions.

3. Login Flow
   - Go back or log out to test Login.
   - Tap "Login".
   - Enter valid credentials.
   - Tap "Login" button.
   - Verify successful login and transition to the Home Page.
   - Test "Forgot Password" or "Guest Login" (Guest Gate) if applicable.

4. Onboarding Instructions & Name Input
   - After signing up, verify the instructional text is clear.
   - Enter the user's name on the Onboarding Name screen.
   - Tap continue.

5. Phenome Selection
   - Verify the Phenome Selection screen loads.
   - Select one or more phenomes to practice.
   - Verify the selections are highlighted/stored.
   - Tap continue.

6. Initial Test / Assessment
   - Verify the TestViewController loads.
   - Follow the instructions to perform the initial speech test.
   - Verify microphone permissions are requested.
   - Complete the test and verify it proceeds to the Last Onboarding screen.

7. Completion
   - Verify the final onboarding success screen.
   - Tap "Get Started" or equivalent.
   - Verify the app navigates to the Home Page.

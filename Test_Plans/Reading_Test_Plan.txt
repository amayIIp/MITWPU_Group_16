Test Plan: Reading

1. Reading Categories
   - Navigate to the Reading tab.
   - Verify the Categories view loads with different reading topics/genres.
   - Select a category and verify it opens a detailed list of reading passages or cards.

2. Reading Selection
   - In the Detail View of a category, scroll through the available reading items (Custom Cards, Random Cards).
   - Verify placeholder texts load properly.
   - Tap on a reading passage to start.

3. Reading Controls
   - Verify the Reading Controls screen loads with the text to read.
   - Tap the microphone/record button to start reading.
   - Read the text aloud. Ensure the app is picking up the audio.
   - Tap stop to finish recording.

4. Reading Results
   - Verify navigation to the Reading Result screen.
   - Check the analyzed results (accuracy, stuttering instances, pace).
   - Verify that specific words or phenomes struggled with are highlighted.
   - Try the "Listen" or "Playback" feature if available to hear your recording.
   - Tap "Done" to return to the categories or home.

5. Custom Workspaces
   - If applicable, test creating or selecting a Custom Workspace for reading.
   - Add custom text and read it.
   - Verify results are generated accurately for custom text.

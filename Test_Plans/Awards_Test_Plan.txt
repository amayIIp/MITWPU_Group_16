Test Plan: Awards

1. Awards Main Screen
   - Navigate to the Awards tab or section.
   - Verify the main view (AwardMainViewController) loads, displaying a summary of earned awards and progress towards new ones.

2. Viewing Awards
   - Scroll through the list or collection of standard awards (AwardStandardCell, AwardCollectionViewCell).
   - Verify that earned awards appear unlocked (colored/bright) and unearned awards appear locked (grayed out or locked icon).
   - Tap on an unlocked award.
   - Verify the Award Detail screen (AwardDetailViewController) opens with the award's name, description, and date earned.
   - Tap on a locked award.
   - Verify the Locked View (LockedViewController) explains what needs to be done to achieve the award.

3. Weekly Challenges
   - Navigate to the Weekly Challenges section (WeeklyChallengesViewController).
   - View the current weekly challenges (WeeklyChallengeCell).
   - Verify progress indicators for each challenge.

4. New Achievements
   - (Simulate or perform an action to earn an award, if possible in the test environment).
   - Verify the Achieved View (AchievedViewController) or a pop-up appears announcing the new award.
   - Return to the Awards screen and verify the award is now unlocked.

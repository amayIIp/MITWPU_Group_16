Test Plan: Conversation

1. Free Conversation Mode
   - Navigate to the Conversation tab.
   - Verify the interface for voice recording loads (VoiceViewController).
   - Tap the microphone button to start a conversation/recording session.

2. Recording and Real-time Feedback
   - Speak freely into the microphone.
   - Verify that any visualizers (waveforms) react to voice input.
   - Tap to pause or stop the recording.

3. Conversation Analysis
   - After stopping, verify that the app processes the audio.
   - Check the resulting analysis for fluency, speech rate, and instances of stuttering.
   - Verify if actionable feedback or a transcript is provided.

4. Saving and History
   - Verify that the conversation session can be saved to the user's history.
   - Navigate away and back to verify state is reset appropriately for a new session.

Test Plan: Exercises

1. Exercise Tab Overview
   - Navigate to the Exercises tab.
   - Verify the main exercise categories and library are visible.
   - Verify the layout matches the designs (collection views load correctly).

2. Exercise Library
   - Tap on the Exercise Library.
   - Verify a list of all available exercises is displayed.
   - Tap an exercise to view its details/instructions.

3. Starting an Exercise
   - Select an exercise (e.g., from a category or the library).
   - Verify the Exercise Instruction screen loads with clear instructions.
   - Tap "Start" or "Begin".

4. During the Exercise (Controls Template)
   - Verify the audio/video recording or playback controls are present and functional.
   - Verify the waveform or audio visualizer responds to voice input.
   - Perform the exercise following the prompts.
   - Stop or finish the exercise.

5. Exercise Results
   - Verify the transition to the Exercise Result screen.
   - Check if the score, feedback, and accuracy are displayed.
   - Test the "Retry" button (should restart the exercise).
   - Test the "Done" or "Back to Home/Exercises" button.

6. Fun Exercises
   - Navigate to the Fun Exercises section.
   - Open a Fun Exercise.
   - Verify it loads correctly and complete the fun activity.
   - Verify results and progress tracking.

7. Audio/Video History
   - If applicable, check the history cells for previous exercises to ensure audio/video snippets are saved and can be replayed.

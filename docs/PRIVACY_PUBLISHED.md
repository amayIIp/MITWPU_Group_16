# What Spasht does with your data

**The short version:** reading-session audio is analysed on your iPhone and deleted right after. Video diary and Story Cubes recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded. With an account, your scores, progress and — if you add one — your profile photo sync to our servers. AI coaching is off until you allow it, and only available with an account. **Guest mode keeps everything on your device.**

## Account information

If you create an account, we collect the following. Signing in with Apple or Google gives us your name and email, and nothing else — if you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Identify your account and sign you back in | Supabase, our backend provider |
| First name | Address you in the app | Supabase |
| Phone number *(optional)* | Only if you choose to add it to your profile | Supabase |
| Profile photo *(optional)* | Only if you choose to add one | Supabase, in private storage only your account can access |

**Guest mode collects none of this.** A guest account is a random identifier generated on your device. It isn't linked to you, and it never leaves the device.

## Practice data

Spasht records what happened in each session — fluency score, accuracy, counts of blocks, repetitions, prolongations and misread words, which words and sounds you found difficult, and your streaks, goals and awards. With an account, this syncs to our servers so it follows you across devices. In guest mode, it stays in a database on your phone only.

Because these figures describe your speech, we treat them as health information. They are used only to run the app for you — never for advertising, never for profiling, and never sold.

## Audio and video

**Reading and practice sessions.** Spasht records audio to measure fluency. It's written to a temporary file on your device, analysed on-device, and deleted as soon as the session ends. It is never uploaded.

**Speech recognition runs entirely on your iPhone**, using Apple's on-device recogniser together with a speech model built into the app. No audio is sent to Apple, to us, or to anyone else.

**Video diary and Story Cubes.** These recordings are saved to private storage on your device only. They are never uploaded. They're included in your iPhone backup if you back up your phone, and they're deleted when you delete your account or the app.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifiers, or anything from the Health app. We don't access your photo library: when you choose a profile photo, iOS gives Spasht only the one photo you pick. We don't track you across other apps or sites, we don't serve ads, and we don't sell personal data.

## Third parties

**Supabase** stores account information, synced practice data and profile photos on our behalf, and does not use them for its own purposes.

**Groq** is an AI provider. Spasht uses it to write the encouraging feedback after a session and to power the conversation practice partner — but **only if you have an account and have said yes.** Before anything is sent, Spasht asks you, names Groq, and explains what's shared. You can change your mind at any time in **Settings → AI Coaching**, and nothing more is sent after you turn it off.

When it's on:

- **After a session**, we send aggregated numbers only — your fluency score, block and repetition percentages, and up to five difficult words. Never your name, email or account ID.
- **During conversation practice**, we send the on-device transcript of what you said. Never audio.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Spasht uses Apple's on-device model first, and Groq is used only if that isn't available. If you don't allow AI coaching, or you're using guest mode, nothing is sent to Groq — feedback is written by the app itself on your device.

**Apple and Google** handle sign-in if you choose it, and share only your name and email with us. Their own privacy policies cover the rest.

**Every provider we share data with** — Supabase, Groq, Apple and Google — is required to protect it to at least the standard described in this policy, and may use it only to provide its service to us.

## How long we keep it, and deleting it

Account data, practice data and your profile photo are kept for as long as your account exists. Session audio is deleted within seconds of analysis.

You can delete your account at any time: **tap your profile picture on the home screen → Profile → Delete Account.** This removes your account, all synced practice history, your profile photo, your on-device data including video diary and Story Cubes recordings, and your Sign in with Apple authorisation. Deletion is immediate and we keep no backup copy.

Guest data exists only on your device, and is removed when you delete the app.

## Your choices

- **AI coaching** — turn it on or off at any time in Settings → AI Coaching.
- **Notifications** — manage them in your iPhone's Settings.
- **Guest mode** — use Spasht without an account to keep everything on your device.
- **Your profile** — edit your name, phone number and photo on the Profile screen.

## Children's privacy

Spasht accounts are for people aged 13 and over. Younger users can use guest mode, which keeps everything on the device and collects nothing. We don't serve ads to anyone, and nobody's data is used beyond providing the app's features. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Most of this is available directly in the app; anything else can go to the email below.
